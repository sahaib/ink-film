# Ink Film — Creative Brief

Copy this, fill what you know, leave the rest blank — Claude will ask about the gaps. The more precise the **Source** and **Forbidden** sections, the better the first storyboard.

---

## 1. Purpose
- **What is this film for?** (a personal piece · a launch · an explainer · an internal story · a pitch)
- **Who watches it, where?** (feed scroll with sound off · a keynote screen · a website hero · a DM to one person)
- **What should they feel or do after 60 seconds?**

## 2. Source (verbatim)
Paste the poem / script / narration exactly as it should appear. Mark lines that must not change. If there are typos you *want* kept, say so.

```
<text here>
```

## 3. Format
- **Length:** 15 s · 30 s · 60 s · 90 s · other (rule of thumb: one plate per 7–12 s; a line needs ≥ 2.5 s on screen)
- **Aspect(s):** 1:1 (1080×1080 · feeds) · 9:16 (1080×1920 · Reels/Shorts/Stories) · 16:9 (1920×1080 · slides, YouTube, web)
- **Loop or end?** seamless loop · hold on the last frame · end card (logo + line)

## 4. Look
- **Preset:** Engraved Cosmos · Blueprint · Risograph · Brand-mapped (see `presets/`) — or "you choose"
- **Three tone words:** e.g. *intimate, cosmic, unresolved* / *precise, confident, calm*
- **References:** links or files (films, illustrations, a post you liked) and what you like about each
- **Recurring anchor motif:** the one thing that returns and changes (the amber point of light at the chest; a thread; a key; a logo mark)

## 5. Brand (corporate only)
- **Colours (hex):** paper/background · ink · accent · accent-hot · cool · dark · ink-on-dark
- **Fonts:** display · text (Google Fonts, or licensed files you can supply)
- **Logo:** SVG/PNG you own; where it may appear (end card only is the default)
- **Claims / numbers on screen:** who signs them off
- **Approvers and deadline**

## 6. Rules
- **Forbidden:** things that must never appear (e.g. *no labels or tooltips*, no specific religious symbols, no faces, no competitor colours)
- **Text policy:** script lines only (default) · plus signature · plus end card · burned-in captions
- **Imagery specificity:** universal symbols · specific tradition/culture (name it) · literal product shots

## 7. Sound
- off by default with a toggle (default) · soundtrack baked into the video · voice-over supplied (file + timing) · silent version only

## 8. Deliverables
- private web page (default) · MP4 with sound · silent MP4 · WAV soundtrack · SRT captions · poster frame · vertical/wide cut-downs

## Worked example — *To My Mind That Forbids* (2026-09-23)

| Field | Answer |
|---|---|
| Purpose | Sahaib's personal poem, told "from the cosmos of space", as vivid and detailed as possible |
| Source | 12 lines verbatim; "/" in the paste read as "I"; "To the rich of I bargain" kept as written |
| Length / aspect | 90 s, 1:1, seamless loop |
| Preset | Engraved Cosmos (ink on aged paper + the void), after Kevin Ngo's "life of a fruit fly" |
| Tone words | intimate · cosmic · unresolved |
| Anchor motif | the Soul: an amber star / flame / point of light at the chest, joined by a thread |
| Decisions | the thunder strikes but the poem still loops (cage cracked, not freed); universal faith imagery (oil lamp, folded hands, thread to a star) |
| Forbidden | **no tooltips / labels in the animation** — only the poem and "— Sahaib" |
| Sound | generated drone / rumble / rain / thunder, off by default; baked into the MP4 |
| Deliverables | private page, 1080 MP4 + silent + WAV |
